# HZ-0E Progress Tracker

Updated: August 5, 2026 (E0-E4 complete. E1-E3: real 4-expert top-1 contract, confirmed collapse-free routing mechanism, and confirmed real gradient-trainability with all 4 auxiliary objectives working as designed -- see prior entries below for full detail. **E4 is a real, honest, largely NEGATIVE result for MoE at this scale, reported plainly, not smoothed over**: a tiny trained low-rank adapter (295K params, ~3% of MoE's total budget) beats MoE's own held-out LM loss; a dense FFN matched to MoE's ACTIVE parameter budget ties MoE at 100 steps and clearly BEATS it at 300 steps; static (non-learned) routing with the identical expert structure is worse than no training at all (confirming learned routing itself is real and load-bearing, even though MoE overall didn't win here); a dense FFN matched to MoE's TOTAL parameter budget performs worst of all (confirming total-param-matched is not the fair comparison; active-param-matched is, and MoE is not ahead there either).

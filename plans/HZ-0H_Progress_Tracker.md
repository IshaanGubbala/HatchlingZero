Full detail in `plans/HZ-0H_H3T_Training_Law_Search.md`. Status: **real, mixed, still open, two real limits found.** Synthetic-gradient predictors trained against real per-position BPTT targets (SG-global) beat a cheaper depth-truncated local-signal version (SG-local) on both alignment and quality, but materially only at a longer (300-step) horizon, not a short pilot. Two attempts to convert that into a compelling production case both failed for the SAME underlying reason (compounding/drift, not a target-quality problem): (1) periodic exact-BPTT recalibration -- quality craters past ~80% synthetic steps, the predictor can't track the true gradient's own drift as the model's weights change; (2) extending SG-global from one shared parameter to all three (`encoder`+`encoder_v`+`decoder`) simultaneously -- WORSE than the single-parameter version (loss 0.7930 vs 0.4203 vs true BPTT's 0.3944) despite each parameter's own signal being individually better than SG-local's. No free lunch measured yet on any dimension tried (parameter count, calibration frequency, or target quality alone). Does NOT block H3 (H3 is blocked on HZ-0G's G1 gate regardless, unrelated), but is meant to settle BEFORE any HZ-0H architecture comparison relies on a specific BDH training recipe. Real next step: larger scale + multi-seed validation before treating any of this as settled -- everything so far is single-seed on a tiny toy model.

## Promotion rule

A BDH mechanism may be proposed for HZ-1 only after passing relevant
oracle/parity tests, beating or materially complementing existing HZ on a
predeclared metric under a matched control, avoiding unacceptable quality/
memory/latency/state regressions, and producing a reproducible report that
records negative and mixed results.

## Ternary guardrail

No result from ternary/1.58-bit training changes the HZ-0H architecture
conclusion unless the corresponding full-precision control is already known.
Treat ternary as an efficiency qualification layer on top of established
architecture evidence, not as the evidence itself.
